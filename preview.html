📖 How to Use FitDiT
  1. Upload Images: Choose a model photo and a garment image, or select from our examples
  2. Select Category: Pick the garment type (Upper-body, Lower-body, or Dresses)
  3. Generate Mask: Click "Step 1: Run Mask" to detect the area to replace
  4. Adjust Mask (Optional): Fine-tune the mask using offset sliders or draw directly on the image
  5. Configure Settings: Choose quality presets or customize advanced parameters
  6. Generate Result: Click "Step 2: Run Try-on" and wait for your amazing result!
💡 Tips for Best Results
  • Use clear, well-lit photos with the person facing forward
  • Garment images should have clean backgrounds
  • Higher resolution settings (1536x2048) give better quality but take longer
  • Adjust mask offsets if the initial mask doesn't fit perfectly
  • Try different seeds to get varied results
⚙️ Parameter Guide
  • Steps (15-30): More steps = better quality but slower. Start with 20.
  • Guidance Scale (1-5): Controls how closely the result follows your input. 2-3 is ideal.
  • Seed: Use -1 for random results, or set a specific number for reproducible results.
  • Resolution: 768x1024 (Fast), 1152x1536 (Balanced), 1536x2048 (Best Quality)
